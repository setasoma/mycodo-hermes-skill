# Cultivation Benchmarks - Knowledge Base

## Yield Benchmarks

### First Flush Yields (Golden Teacher)
- **Golden Teacher first flush**: ~337g wet -> ~26g dry (~7.6% wet-to-dry conversion ratio)
- **Dehydration parameters**: 120°F for 24 hours

## Substrate Innovations

### Laboratory Cultivation
- **Coffee filter paper** as accessible substrate for slime mold cultivation (Badhamia utricularis) - alternative to agar plates
- **Soft agar gel + coffee filter paper** combination effective for home lab slime mold growth

## Handling Best Practices

### All-in-One Grow Bags
- **Over-handling penalty**: Breaking up mycelium multiple times during colonization causes cake shrinkage and complete fruiting failure — minimize disruption once colonization establishes
- **Insufficient FAE**: AIO bags dumped to tubs without proper fresh air exchange result in no fruiting after 1+ month despite full colonization

### Species-Specific Substrate Preferences
- **Lion's Mane on BRF/verm**: Slow fruiting (10+ days) on standard PF Tek substrate — consider hardwood supplementation for Hericium species optimization
- **Turkey tail timeline**: 5-6 week fruiting timeline from inoculation to harvest — longer than typical cubensis varieties
