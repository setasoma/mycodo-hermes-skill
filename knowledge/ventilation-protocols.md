# Ventilation Protocols for CO2 Management

Aggressive and passive techniques for Lion's Mane fruiting phase.

---

## Purge Protocols

### Continuous Fan + Window Open
- **Efficacy:** ~688 ppm CO2 reduction (1394 -> 706 ppm) in ~100 minutes
- **Rate:** ~450 ppm/hour CO2 clearance with sustained air exchange
- **Trade-off:** Humidity crash 95.5% -> 53% (manageable with manual rehumidification)

### Tent Door Open Technique
- **Efficacy:** ~371 ppm CO2 reduction (1426 -> 1055 ppm) in 29 minutes
- **Speed:** ~768 ppm/hour clearance rate — fastest method observed
- **Application:** Emergency CO2 management when continuous fan insufficient

## CO2 Accumulation Rates

- **Fruiting phase:** ~160-170 ppm/hour when fan off (active metabolic respiration)
- **Critical threshold:** >1000 ppm triggers alert; >1400 ppm requires emergency intervention
- **Acceptable target:** <800 ppm for healthy fruit development

## Humidity Trade-Offs

- **Priority hierarchy:** CO2 reduction > humidity preservation during critical phases
- **Recovery:** Manual spray effective for rehumidification post-purge
- **Buffer requirement:** Minimum 88% humidity to allow fan operation per fruiting rules

## Integration with AC

- AC + open tent door + continuous fan = maximum CO2 clearance + temperature control
- Multi-modal ventilation critical for high-respiration fruiting stages

## Late-Stage Fruiting CO2 & Humidity Dynamics

- **Late-stage fruiting CO2 accumulation dramatically higher:** ~670 ppm/hour when fan off. Far exceeds early-fruiting rate of ~160-170 ppm/hour — mature fruiting bodies produce significantly more CO2.
- **Short-duration fan-off CO2 climb:** ~325 ppm in 20 minutes (577 -> 902 ppm), ~15-16 ppm/min. Even brief fan pauses during late-stage fruiting allow rapid CO2 buildup.
- **Humidity recovery speed:** 65.7% -> 99.1% in ~20 minutes with manual humidification. Rapid recovery possible when operator present and fan paused.
- **Operator-managed humidification + continuous fan validated:** CO2 stable at ~545 ppm with continuous fan ON; operator handles manual humidification during breaks. Successfully balances CO2 control and humidity for late-stage fruiting.
